Scriptorium of the Starfall

An elderly scribe sits on a turning astrolabe and copies the sky into a notebook the size of his hand, while a colossal codex empties its pages down into a canyon river running gold and cosmic blue.

Light

Two sources and no neutral ground between them: the codex and the river throw a cold, magical glow up the canyon walls; the horizon answers with warm orange across every right-hand edge. Nebula light fills whatever is left.

Technique

Built as a painted plate rather than a diagram: layered gradient washes, additive glow, grain, and particle work for the fall, the sparks and the drift. Nothing here is an image file — the frame is composed and rendered in the browser each time you open it.
